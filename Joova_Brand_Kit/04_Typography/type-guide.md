# Joova typography

- **Syne ExtraBold (800)**: headlines and the joova. wordmark. Tight letter spacing (about -2%).
- **DM Sans**: everything else. Regular 400 body, Medium 500, SemiBold 600 labels, Bold 700 buttons.
- Both are free under the SIL Open Font License (license files included). Also available at fonts.google.com.
- Never retype the logo in a font; always use the files in 02_Logo.
