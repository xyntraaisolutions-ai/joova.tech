# Joova color guide

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | #15171C | 21, 23, 28 | Text, dark sections, logo |
| Paper | #F6F3EE | 246, 243, 238 | Backgrounds, box back |
| Coral | #F26B4E | 242, 107, 78 | Buttons, accents, the dot in joova. |
| Coral text | #B8402A | 184, 64, 42 | Small coral text on light backgrounds |
| Stone | #E5DFD6 | 229, 223, 214 | Lines, cards |
| Muted | #5F5A53 | 95, 90, 83 | Secondary text |

Proportion: about 60% paper, 25% ink, 10% coral, 5% stone.

Readable pairs: ink on paper, coral text (#B8402A) on paper, ink on coral, paper on ink. Do not use white text on bright coral for small text.

Print: match Coral #F26B4E to a Pantone chip with the box printer and approve a printed proof [CONFIRM]. CMYK values come from the printer's proof, not from a screen.

Box colors, band colors and ring finishes: see joova-colors.css.
